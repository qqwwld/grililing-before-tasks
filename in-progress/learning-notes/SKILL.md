---
name: learning-notes
description: Create Q&A notes from learning records, preserving the actual progression, important knowledge, learner doubts, and changes in understanding. Use only when the learner requests notes or updates.
---

Help the learner quickly rebuild their understanding after forgetting. Write in the learner's language.

1. **Align before drafting.** Extract the purpose, scope, materials, and agreed preferences from context. Discuss only unresolved choices that affect recall, asking one focused question at a time. Confirm the approach and stop when aligned; do not re-ask settled questions.

2. **Ground the notes.** Use accessible learning records and referenced materials. State coverage briefly and disclose missing history. Never invent conversations, citations, beliefs, emotions, or progress. Retain available source links or file/section references.

3. **Follow the learning path.** Preserve the actual lesson progression, from foundations to increasingly complex ideas. Select important knowledge, essential reasoning, meaningful questions, and corrections; remove repetition and logistical chatter. Keep significant detours and revisions where they occurred rather than regrouping the main notes by a knowledge taxonomy.

4. **Reconstruct through Q&A.** Use self-contained questions with a short core answer and only the explanation, derivation, or example needed to recall the reasoning. Preserve actual learner doubts and label generated review questions. Where supported by the records, show what the learner already knew, what blocked understanding, which explanation or inference helped, and how their understanding changed. Do not force these fields onto every entry or infer unstated inner experiences.

5. **Preserve uncertainty.** Include supported corrections alongside important misconceptions; never present an error as the accepted answer. Mark unresolved doubts and keep unanswered exercises pending. Do not complete hints or derive pending solutions while taking notes.

6. **Summarize and check.** End with a compact knowledge framework showing key concepts, dependencies, relationships, and applicability. Check coverage, the learning order, self-contained answers, and consistency with the records. Avoid repeating the full Q&A in the summary.

Default to Markdown: title and scope, Q&A along the learning progression, then the knowledge framework. Follow local note conventions. Save files only when requested; preserve existing content when updating.
